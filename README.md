# Mirai

### Explore. Stream. Read. Anywhere.

---

A sleek, lightweight cross-platform anime and manga client built for speed and simplicity. Engineered to deliver a fluid, high-performance experience without intrusive ads, tracking clutter, or unnecessary interruptions. Stream high-definition anime, read your favorite manga and manhwa, sync your watchlist across devices, and download media for offline access on Android, iOS, and Windows.

</div>

## ✨ Key Attributes

### 🔍 Discovery & Content Hub
*   **Anime & Manga in One Place:** Seamlessly switch between streaming anime episodes and reading manga or manhwa chapters within a unified interface.
*   **Intuitive Browsing:** Instantly explore trending titles, seasonal releases, popular franchises, and recently updated chapters.
*   **Smart Search & Filtering:** Quickly find series by title, genre, format, or release status with comprehensive metadata, user ratings, and synopses.
*   **Multi-Subtitle Support:** Choose your preferred viewing experience with multi-sub options across available streaming providers.

### 📖 Immersive Reading Experience
*   **Built-in Manga Reader:** Smooth page-turning, chapter navigation, and customizable viewing modes tailored for manga, manhwa, and webtoons.
*   **Reading Progress Tracking:** Automatically remembers the exact chapter and page where you left off.

### 🌐 Cross-Platform Performance
*   **Universal Compatibility:** Native support across **Android**, **iOS / iPadOS**, and **Windows Desktop**.
*   **Optimized & Lightweight:** Designed to consume minimal system memory while maintaining snappy responsiveness and ultra-fast page loading.
*   **Reduced Buffering:** Enhanced network management to ensure smooth video playback and rapid image rendering even on slower connections.

### 🎨 Clean UI & Experience
*   **Completely Ad-Free:** Zero third-party ads, pop-ups, or banners for an uninterrupted viewing and reading session.
*   **Custom Dark Themes:** Aesthetic dark-mode and custom color themes designed to eliminate eye strain during late-night sessions.
*   **Continue Watching & Reading:** Instant access to your recent anime history and reading queue right from the home screen.

### 📥 Offline Access & Downloads
*   **Direct Media Downloading:** Save full anime episodes directly to your storage for offline playback anytime.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **iOS & iPadOS Download Notice:**  
> Offline video playback on iOS devices requires an external media player application (such as **VLC for Mobile**).

### ☁️ Cloud Sync & Account Security
*   **Cross-Device Cloud Sync:** Powered by a secure Firestore backend so your watchlist, bookmarks, and watch/read progress sync seamlessly across mobile and desktop.
*   **Secure Authentication:** Keep your library personalized and protected through simple account authentication.
*   **Instant Manual Backup:** One-tap save controls directly on media info and watchlist screens to keep your progress updated on demand.
